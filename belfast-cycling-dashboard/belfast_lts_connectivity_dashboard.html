Detour Tolerance ? What is detour tolerance?

A route is considered "connected" only if a low-stress path exists that doesn't exceed the shortest possible route by more than this percentage. For example, at 125%, a 10 km shortest route allows a low-stress alternative up to 12.5 km.

For short trips, an absolute tolerance of 530 m (0.33 miles) is also applied, whichever is more generous.

Recommended: 125% (Mekuria et al. 2012 default)

About

Two points are connected at a given LTS level if a path exists using only edges ≤ that stress level, without exceeding the detour tolerance. Data: Census 2021 OD flows (ODWP01), OSM road network.

Methodology: Mekuria, Furth & Nixon (2012).
